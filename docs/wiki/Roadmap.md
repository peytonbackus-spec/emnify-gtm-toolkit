# Roadmap

A plan to argue with. Targets are set with the Revenue Operations Lead in week two from real data; none is a number yet.

- **Weeks 1-4:** walk the existing flows, agree the revenue metric and write permissions, audit enrichment precision per provider, then **ship one small dry-run-first enrichment run** with approval on every write.
- **Months 2-3:** calibrate scoring on won and lost accounts, run the coverage model on real go-live history, turn contracts into approval-gated n8n workflows, launch one outbound motion in one vertical, add consumption qualification fields to opportunities.
- **Months 4-6:** compare signal-triggered with list-based cohorts, compare expected with realized usage, move at most one agent task to spot-check with evidence, and cut anything that does not earn its place.

What would change the plan: enrichment already good, revenue concentrated outside the listed verticals, or no usable device and go-live history. Full version in `ROADMAP.md`.
